# Glossary

Domain terms used across Amana, written for non-technical readers.

## Escrow
Funds held by the platform on behalf of a trade. When a buyer pays, the money goes into escrow rather than straight to the seller. It is released to the seller only when the agreed conditions are met, or refunded to the buyer if the trade fails. Escrow is what makes buying and selling on Amana safe for both sides.

## Loss-ratio
The share of a party's trades that ended in a loss — a refund, a lost dispute, or a clawback. A low loss-ratio means few problem trades and a good standing on the platform. Buyers can use a seller's loss-ratio as a signal of reliability.

## PoD (Proof of Delivery)
Evidence that a seller delivered the goods or service. Examples: a tracking number, a delivery photo, a signed receipt, or a timestamped handover record. Strong PoD is the seller's main protection in a dispute.

## Clawback
A reversal of funds that have already been paid out. Clawbacks are used when fraud or a serious breach is proven — for example a forged PoD or a fake listing. They return money to the wronged party and may come with account restrictions.

## Streams
Continuous, per-second payouts for ongoing services, instead of a single lump sum. Streams suit subscriptions, retainers, and long-running work: the seller is paid as the work progresses, and either side can stop the stream when the arrangement ends.

## Related terms

- **Mediator** — a neutral operator who resolves disputed trades. See the [Mediator Handbook](mediator/handbook.md).
- **Release condition** — the rule that decides when escrow pays out, agreed when the trade starts.
- **Dispute** — a trade both parties cannot settle, escalated to mediation.
- **Trade lifecycle** — the full path of a trade, from listing to release. See the [trade-lifecycle diagram](diagrams/trade-lifecycle.md).
