# Seller Guide

This guide is for **sellers** — people who list goods or services on Amana and get paid through escrow. No technical knowledge is required.

## What Amana does for you

Amana holds the buyer's money in **escrow** until the trade is complete. That means you can ship or deliver with confidence: the funds are already committed and locked, and they are released to you once the agreed conditions are met.

## Before you start

- A funded wallet or connected payment method so you can receive payouts.
- A clear description of what you are selling, the price, and the delivery terms.
- A delivery window you can realistically meet.

## Step 1 — Create a listing

1. Open **Create listing** from your dashboard.
2. Enter a title, description, price, and currency.
3. Set the **delivery window** (how long the buyer should expect to wait).
4. Choose your **release condition** — for example, buyer confirmation on delivery, or a tracking number plus a waiting period.
5. Publish. Your listing becomes visible to buyers.

## Step 2 — Agree the trade

When a buyer starts a trade, an **escrow** is opened and the buyer's funds are locked. You will see the trade in **My trades** with the status `Funded`.

Do not ship or deliver until the trade shows `Funded`. If it still shows `Awaiting payment`, the buyer has not paid yet.

## Step 3 — Deliver

1. Fulfil the order within the agreed delivery window.
2. Upload any proof the trade requires — for example a tracking number, delivery photo, or signed receipt. This is your **Proof of Delivery (PoD)**.
3. Mark the trade as **Delivered**.

Good PoD is your best protection. Clear, timestamped evidence makes disputes quick to resolve in your favour.

## Step 4 — Get paid

- If the buyer confirms receipt, escrow releases the funds to you automatically.
- If the buyer does nothing, the funds release to you after the waiting period once you have submitted valid PoD.
- Payouts arrive in your wallet or linked account. Timing depends on your payout method.

## If something goes wrong

- **Buyer is unresponsive.** Submit your PoD and wait out the release window. The funds release to you automatically.
- **Buyer disputes the trade.** A **mediator** reviews the evidence from both sides. Upload everything you have: tracking, photos, messages, receipts.
- **You cannot deliver in time.** Message the buyer as early as possible. Agreeing an extension is far better than a dispute.

## Disputes and mediation

If a trade is disputed, it moves to mediation. A neutral mediator examines the escrow, your PoD, and the buyer's claims, then decides how the funds are split. See the [Mediator Handbook](../mediator/handbook.md) for how that process works.

## Understanding your numbers

- **Escrow** — funds held safely until the trade completes.
- **Loss-ratio** — the share of your trades that end in a loss (refund, dispute loss, or clawback). A low loss-ratio keeps your account in good standing.
- **Clawback** — a reversal of funds already paid to you, used when fraud or a serious breach is proven.
- **Streams** — continuous, per-second payouts for ongoing services instead of a single lump sum.

See the full [Glossary](../glossary.md) for all terms.

## Tips for sellers

- Keep listings accurate; mismatched descriptions cause most disputes.
- Always upload PoD, even when the buyer seems happy.
- Respond to buyer messages within a day.
- Never move a trade off-platform — you lose escrow protection.
