# Buyer Guide

This guide is for **buyers** — people who purchase goods or services on Amana. No technical knowledge is required.

## What Amana does for you

When you buy on Amana, your money goes into **escrow**: it is locked and held by the platform, not handed straight to the seller. The seller only gets paid once the trade is completed as agreed. If something goes wrong, a **mediator** can step in.

## Step 1 — Find what you need

1. Browse or search listings.
2. Open a listing and read the description, price, delivery window, and release conditions carefully.
3. Check the seller's history and **loss-ratio** — a low loss-ratio means few problem trades.

## Step 2 — Start a trade

1. Select **Buy** on the listing.
2. Confirm the price, currency, and delivery window.
3. Pay. Your funds move into escrow and the trade status becomes `Funded`.

The seller can now see that the money is secured and will begin delivery.

## Step 3 — Wait for delivery

Track the trade in **My trades**. The seller will mark it **Delivered** and upload **Proof of Delivery (PoD)** — for example a tracking number or delivery photo.

## Step 4 — Confirm and release

1. When your item arrives and matches the description, open the trade and select **Confirm receipt**.
2. Escrow releases the funds to the seller.

If you do nothing, the funds release to the seller automatically after the waiting period once valid PoD is submitted. So confirm promptly if you are happy — or raise a dispute if you are not.

## If something goes wrong

- **Item never arrives.** Do not confirm. Open a dispute before the release window closes.
- **Item is not as described.** Open a dispute and upload photos and messages as evidence.
- **Seller asks you to pay off-platform.** Refuse. Off-platform payments are not protected by escrow.

## Disputes and mediation

When you open a dispute, the trade goes to a neutral **mediator**. They review the escrow, the seller's PoD, and your evidence, then decide how the funds are split. See the [Mediator Handbook](../mediator/handbook.md) for the full process.

## Understanding your numbers

- **Escrow** — your funds, held safely until the trade completes.
- **PoD (Proof of Delivery)** — evidence the seller delivered, such as tracking or photos.
- **Clawback** — a reversal of funds already paid out, used when fraud is proven.
- **Streams** — continuous per-second payments for ongoing services.

See the full [Glossary](../glossary.md) for all terms.

## Tips for buyers

- Read the release conditions before you pay.
- Keep all communication on-platform.
- Confirm receipt promptly when you are satisfied.
- Raise a dispute early — waiting until after release makes recovery much harder.
